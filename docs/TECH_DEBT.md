## Deliberately not "fixed" (client decisions)

- **Double booking is allowed** — slots are never consumed by existing bookings.
- **Phone auth is India-only** — international numbers are out of scope.
- **Email confirmation is required** — never auto-confirm signups; fix messages, not the rule.
